The Decision

You are not only asking whether you are ahead. You are asking whether the game is valuable enough, volatile enough, and urgent enough to change the stakes now.

The Simple Answer

The cube turns a position into a decision about risk, reward, and timing.

Key idea

A strong cube decision is not just about who is winning. It is about what can happen before you get another chance to double.

A Clean Model

Start with a simplified money-game model: no gammons and no future recube. In that model, a take begins around 25%.

Take point ~= risk / (risk + reward)

What Changes the Answer?

Getting 10 in the Zone can turn an Active Builder into an immediate attacking threat, making the position more volatile before your next chance to double.

  • Race lead.
  • Gammons.
  • Threats.
  • Market losers.
  • Cube ownership.
  • Match score.

Engine settings

Practical Checklist

  1. Am I ahead enough?
  2. Can I lose my market?
  3. How many gammons are in the position?
  4. What does the receiver own after taking?
  5. Does match score change the value of gammons or cube ownership?
