About Backgammon Simplified

Backgammon Simplified is an open-source project for understanding the game and making better decisions.

The idea is simple:

Come with a question. Leave with a clear mental model you can use at the board.

We try to make difficult ideas clearer, build useful tools, and help more people get excited about backgammon.

Why We Built This

I started this project while trying to understand my own blunders. The engine gave me the best moves. A friend who is a great player said, “Eventually, you’ll start to see it.”

I wanted to know what I was supposed to see, as I asked questions like:

> Why is 25% often called the take point… and yet it rarely ever is?

> When should I run a lone back checker, move it up, or leave it where it is?

> Why is breaking an anchor brilliant one game and a blunder the next?

> What can one position teach me about the thousands I’ll see later?

// goal: clarity over perfection

Backgammon Simplified is my attempt to answer questions with real positions, clear explanations, and evidence instead of folklore.
